I do not talk to anyone over email. Email is quite flawed (as a protocol) for private or sensitive communications. End-to-end encrypted instant messengers like these are far more suitable for private or sensitive communications, have good standing with the digital privacy community, and have robust and competent encryption implementations behind them.

### Regarding PGP
PGP does do not have *perfect forward secrecy* or *break-in recovery*, isn't convenient to use (for most people) in the context of messaging (even when considering current client-facing implementations like Proton Mail) because of manual key management, and has two different, major specifications that potentially create [conflict and confusion](https://blog.pgpkeys.eu/critique-critique.html). These are some of the reasons I (personally) will not use PGP for specifically encrypted messaging.
